WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN

What would make this worth pursuing?

Evidence that customers repeatedly buy the outcome is a starting point. Your opportunity also needs a reachable buyer, a reason they would choose you, and delivery economics that work.

  • A specific customer and existing alternative.
  • A costly or important job they need done.
  • Evidence of willingness to pay for your proposed offer.
  • A repeatable way to reach and retain customers.
  • A delivery method you can own and improve.

If those are unknown, the next useful result is a focused research or paid-test plan. A map, successful-company story, or estimated industry margin cannot settle those questions alone.
