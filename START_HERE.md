# Understand the project in two minutes

Open the [live website](https://aipi510-module3-main-street.vercel.app/). Choose a category and county, inspect the history, and run an estimate.

The project predicts next-year establishment counts with a Ridge model. Training uses 2021 targets, validation uses 2022 targets, and test uses 2023 targets. The 2024 estimate is historical and unverified.

Verified components include R2 ingestion, chronological training, MLflow, Flask `/predict`, Docker, public deployment, 61 tests, and 12 reproduction checks.
